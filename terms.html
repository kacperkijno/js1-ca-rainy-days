Terms and conditions

The rules for shopping at Rainy Days – orders, payment, delivery and returns.

This is a student project for Noroff (JavaScript 1). Rainy Days is not a real shop. The text on this page is placeholder content drafted with the help of AI (Claude, Anthropic) and edited by me – see AI_LOG.md in the repository.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to all purchases made in the Rainy Days online store. By placing an order you accept these terms.

2. Orders

Your order is binding once you receive an order confirmation by email. We reserve the right to cancel orders if a product is out of stock or a price is clearly wrong.

3. Prices and payment

All prices include VAT. You can pay by card, Vipps, Klarna or PayPal. The amount is charged when your order is shipped.

4. Delivery

Standard delivery takes 5–7 working days, express delivery 1–2 working days. You can also pick up your order free of charge in one of our stores in Bergen, Haugesund or Stavanger.

5. Returns and right of withdrawal

You have 30 days to return unused products with the tags still on. Contact us before sending a return. We refund the purchase price within 14 days after we receive the product.

6. Warranty and complaints

If a product has a defect, you can make a complaint within the time limits of the Norwegian Consumer Purchases Act (forbrukerkjøpsloven).

7. Contact

Questions about an order? Visit one of our stores or send us an email at hello@rainydays.example.
