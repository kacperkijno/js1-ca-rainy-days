Privacy policy

How Rainy Days collects, uses and protects your personal data.

This is a student project for Noroff (JavaScript 1). Rainy Days is not a real shop. The text on this page is placeholder content drafted with the help of AI (Claude, Anthropic) and edited by me – see AI_LOG.md in the repository.

1. Who is responsible

Rainy Days is responsible for the personal data collected through this website.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you enter at checkout (name, email, phone, address)
  • Order history
  • Your email address if you sign up for the newsletter

3. Why we use it

We use your data to deliver orders, send order confirmations, handle returns and – only if you agree – send you news and offers.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking cookies. Your shopping cart is saved in your own browser (localStorage) so it stays there when you move between pages. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep it

Order data is kept as long as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email.

6. Your rights

You have the right to see, correct and delete your personal data, and to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
